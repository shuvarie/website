Shuvarie is configured with [KDL](https://kdl.dev) files. Workspace files
let a project tune its own agent; the global file holds your personal
defaults.

- [Configuration files](/docs/configuration/files/) — where they live and
  how the layers combine
- [Interface & themes](/docs/configuration/ui/) — `ui`, `title`, `image`
  and `themes`
- [Agent & context](/docs/configuration/agent/) — `agent`, `retry`,
  `context`
- [Skills](/docs/configuration/skills/) — reusable prompts and procedures
- [Tools & integrations](/docs/configuration/tools/) — `tools` (web
  search, custom stdio tools, MCP servers), `shell`, `lsp`
- [Attachments](/docs/configuration/attachments/) — documents, images and
  the office converter
- [Providers & models](/docs/configuration/providers/) —
  `connections.kdl`, `default-providers`, `registries`, `embedding`
- [Scenes](/docs/configuration/scenes/) — mission profiles for the agent
- [Permissions](/docs/configuration/permissions/) — what the agent may
  touch
- [Decisions](/docs/configuration/decisions/) — decision models and option
  ranking
